 **Help me analyze my interview with \[INTERVIEWEE] from \[COMPANY/ORGANIZATION].**

 1. Review the full transcript or recording of our conversation.
 2. Identify and list each question I was asked.
 3. Summarize my responses to those questions.
 4. Evaluate my performance:

    * **Strengths:** What I did or said well (e.g. clarity, structure, examples, rapport).
    * **Weaknesses:** Which answers or behaviors could be improved (e.g. pacing, depth, confidence).
 5. Assess my interaction style (e.g. tone, listening skills, follow‑up questions).
 6. Recommend concrete tweaks for future interviews—better phrasing, examples to add, questions to ask, or strategies to strengthen.

**How to use:**

* Swap in the interviewee’s name and company.
* Provide the transcript or key excerpts.
* Optionally specify the role you were interviewing for or particular areas you’re most concerned about.
